# EventArgs LLC Employee Policy Handbook

## 1. Remote Work Policy
All EventArgs LLC team members are eligible for flexible remote work. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST. Equipment allowances up to $1,500 per year are provided for home office ergonomics.

## 2. Expense Reimbursement
Travel and client entertainment expenses must be submitted within 30 days of occurrence via the internal portal. Receipts are mandatory for any expense exceeding $25. Pre-approval from department lead is required for expenses over $500.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accruing monthly. A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can roll over into the next calendar year.
